Which MP3 Quality Should You Choose?

Setting Best for Approx. size per minute
MP3 320 kbps Music, best quality 2.4 MB
MP3 192 kbps Everyday listening 1.4 MB
MP3 128 kbps Speech, podcasts, small files 1 MB
WAV 16-bit Editing, no compression about 10 MB

An MP3 can never sound better than the audio in the original video. A higher bitrate keeps more of that audio but does not improve it.
