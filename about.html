Our Vision & Architecture

Building the Fastest, Most Private Web Tools

ToolStacker was founded with a singular conviction: document processing should never require surrendering your personal privacy to cloud servers.

100% In-Browser Privacy

Every PDF merge, image crop, stamp, and metadata cleanse is computed locally via modern WebAssembly and JavaScript. Zero files are sent to remote servers.

Zero Wait Times

Without server upload pipelines or queues, conversions complete in milliseconds, saving bandwidth and delivering instant download links.

Completely Free & Open

No mandatory signups, no watermarks, no hidden paywalls, and no arbitrary limits. Practical productivity for professionals, students, and businesses.

Why We Built ToolStacker

Most conventional online PDF and image conversion platforms follow a model where your confidential files—contracts, tax returns, personal identification, and proprietary photographs—must be uploaded to third-party cloud servers. Even with promises of scheduled deletions, data breaches and unauthorized retention remain persistent threats.

We leveraged breakthroughs in modern browser technology, including WebAssembly and high-performance client libraries (like pdf-lib, pdf.js, and jsPDF), to move all computational processing directly into your browser tab.

The result is a comprehensive suite of professional utilities that offers the ease of a web application with the confidentiality of desktop software.
