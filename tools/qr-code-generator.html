Static vs. Dynamic QR Codes

ToolStacker: 100% Static & Permanent

Every QR code generated on ToolStacker encodes your data (URL, WiFi, vCard, Location) directly into the black and white dot pattern without any middleman servers.

  • Never Expires: Scannable forever, even in 50 years.
  • Zero Tracking & Total Privacy: 100% computed in your browser. Data is never saved or logged.
  • Unlimited Scans: No monthly quotas or paywalls after printing.

What Are Dynamic QR Codes?

Dynamic QR codes route through an external redirect domain (e.g. qr.service.com/redirect123). While they allow changing the destination link later, they stop working if the provider changes pricing or shuts down. Static codes never break.
