Formula & Guide

How to Calculate Discount Percentages & Sales Tax

Calculating the true savings during shopping sales, Black Friday promotions, or retail markdowns involves two straightforward steps: calculating the markdown value and then computing the tax on the discounted subtotal.

Discount Formula

Savings = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)

Example: A $120 jacket with a 25% discount gives $120 × 0.25 = $30 in total savings. The discounted subtotal is $90.

Sales Tax Formula

Total Tax = Discounted Subtotal × (Tax Rate % ÷ 100)

Tax is calculated on the sale price ($90), not the original MSRP. At an 8% tax rate: $90 × 0.08 = $7.20 tax. Final price = $97.20.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do stacked or double discounts work?

Stacked discounts are applied consecutively. For example, if an item is 20% off and you have an additional 10% coupon, the 10% is deducted from the already-discounted price, not the original price. This results in an effective 28% total discount rather than a combined 30%.

Is sales tax calculated before or after discounts?

In most jurisdictions, sales tax is computed on the final discounted transaction price paid by the customer. However, manufacturer rebates or gift cards may have distinct local tax rules.
